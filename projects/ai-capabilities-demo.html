AI Capabilities Demo — Commercial Sales Enablement Platform

Production web application showcasing AI capabilities for enterprise healthcare customers — a core commercial sales tool

Azure Speech Services rPPG Voice Biomarker AI JavaScript REST APIs Web Frontend Azure Cognitive Services

Overview

Built a production-grade web application to demonstrate AI capabilities to enterprise healthcare customers during commercial sales engagements. The platform brings cutting-edge AI research into a live, interactive demo experience — turning abstract AI concepts into tangible, visceral demonstrations that enterprise buyers can see and touch during a sales meeting. The application directly supported customer acquisition by serving as the primary AI capabilities showcase for Teladoc Health's commercial team, integrating multiple advanced AI modalities into a single polished platform.

The Challenge

Enterprise healthcare customers need visceral proof of AI capabilities, not slide decks. A description of what AI can do is far less compelling than a live demonstration showing it happening in the room. The demo needed to showcase multiple advanced AI modalities reliably under pressure — in live sales meetings where technical glitches mean lost deals and lost credibility. Each capability also required integrating a different third-party AI vendor, each with its own API contract, reliability characteristics, and quirks. The system needed to present these disparate integrations as a cohesive, polished experience that a commercial sales team with no technical background could operate confidently.

Technical Approach

  • Azure Speech Cognitive Services: real-time speech-to-text and text-to-speech demonstration with live transcript display and voice synthesis
  • Voice biomarker detection: integration with specialist vendor AI detecting vocal biomarkers associated with aggression and depression from live audio streams
  • rPPG (remote photoplethysmography): contactless vital sign estimation using a standard webcam — measuring heart rate and respiratory signals from subtle facial color changes
  • Led all vendor integrations and API contracts — coordinating technical requirements, authentication, and data flows across multiple AI providers
  • Built for reliability in live demo conditions with graceful fallbacks and error handling that maintains a professional experience even when an external API has issues
  • Deployed as primary sales tool used by commercial teams in customer meetings — designed for non-technical operators with intuitive controls and instant setup
