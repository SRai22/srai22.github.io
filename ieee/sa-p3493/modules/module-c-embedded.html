MODULE C: SCREENING CLINICAL PROCESS MODEL

IEEE P3493.1 Standard for Cancer Screening

Draft Version: 1.00 | Date: 2026-02-02 | Status: Complete with AI Integration (Clause 5.7)

Module Overview

Module C defines the comprehensive clinical process model for cancer screening, establishing normative requirements for how screening workflows should be structured, documented, and linked to resulting diagnostic and treatment episodes.

Key Components:

  • Clause 5.1: Ontological Foundation - Screening as Clinical Process
  • Clause 5.2: Stage-Based Workflow Model (4 normative stages)
  • Clause 5.3: Conditional Episode Derivation and Linkage Patterns
  • Clause 5.4: Health State Transitions in Cancer Screening
  • Clause 5.5: Clinical Decision Points and Branching Logic
  • Clause 5.6: Temporal Requirements and Time Constraints
  • Clause 5.7: AI and Automated Systems in Cancer Screening

This module provides the technical framework for modeling cancer screening as a structured clinical process, distinguishing it from episodic care while specifying how diagnostic and treatment episodes are conditionally derived from screening findings. It includes comprehensive requirements for AI integration, health state tracking, and temporal constraints.

Technical Highlights

📊

4-Stage Workflow Model

Normative stages covering process initiation, screening execution, result communication, and transition logic with conditional episode derivation.

🔗

Linkage Patterns

Two normative patterns (A: Co-Located, B: Separate) for linking screening processes to diagnostic episodes with full traceability metadata.

🏥

Health State Transitions

12 standardized health states (HS-01 through HS-12) tracking subjects from eligibility through diagnostic resolution or loss to follow-up.

⏱️

Temporal Requirements

Specific time constraints (T1-T11) for critical transitions including result communication (5 days for positive), referral acceptance (14 days).

🤖

AI Integration (Clause 5.7)

Framework for incorporating automated medical devices and AI systems with accountability requirements and human oversight specifications.

🎯

Clinical Decision Points

Structured branching logic at key decision points with documentation requirements for quality measurement and process improvement.

ISO 13940:2015 Alignment

Module C builds upon the terminology framework established in Module B, applying ISO 13940:2015 concepts to cancer screening workflows:

  • Clinical Process vs. Episode of Care distinction (ontological foundation)
  • Health State tracking throughout screening lifecycle
  • Healthcare Activities composition within normative stages
  • Care Period Mandates and responsibility transfer (Pattern B linkage)
  • Health Threads for longitudinal continuity across screening, diagnosis, and treatment
  • Observed Conditions triggering diagnostic episode initiation (HS-07)

This alignment ensures semantic interoperability with broader healthcare informatics standards and supports consistent data recycling per the P3493.1 scope.
